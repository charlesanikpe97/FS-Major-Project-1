About Me

A bit more

about me.

My name's Charles Anikpe, I was born and brought up in Rome, Italy. I’m an aspiring Software Development Engineer driven by curiosity, persistence, and a genuine desire to build things that solve real problems.

I’m an IT Escalation Engineer at Amazon, currently working toward transitioning into a Software Development Engineer (SDE) role within the company. I began programming about five years ago during the pandemic, learning through Codecademy, YouTube, and Udemy. At the time, I was working as an Amazon Warehouse Associate and was fascinated by programming, although I wasn’t yet sure how to turn that interest into a career. Rather than moving directly into web development, I focused first on building a stronger foundation in IT. I earned several CompTIA and AWS certifications, which helped me transition into a corporate IT role at Amazon. I started as a Helpdesk Associate and progressed to IT Escalation Engineer, a role at the same internal level as an entry-level SDE. My focus now is on developing the technical depth, problem-solving skills, and mental models required to succeed as a software engineer. I previously completed a Frontend Web Development bootcamp through OpenClassrooms and am now expanding my backend knowledge through Codecademy’s Full-Stack Web Development program. Alongside the program, I continue to self-study, explore key topics in greater depth, and strengthen my skills through practical projects.
